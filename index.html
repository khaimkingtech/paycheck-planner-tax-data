Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. Payperity has no accounts and never sends your numbers anywhere.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Open Backup & export and tap Export backup (.json), then save it to Files, iCloud Drive or email it to yourself. On the new device, tap Restore a backup and choose that file. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export a backup first.

How are taxes calculated?

Payperity estimates federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and state income tax for all 50 states and DC using the current year's published brackets, deductions and limits. Figures update automatically each January. Results are estimates for budgeting, not tax advice.

Can I use my own state tax rate?

Yes. Under Income & taxes, enter Your state rate to override the state brackets.

Can I import my bank transactions?

Yes. Download a CSV from your bank's website, then use Import from your bank in the Money log. The file is read on your device only.

Does Payperity give financial advice?

No. It shows budgeting guidelines (like 50/30/20) and where your plan goes over them. For personal advice, talk to a qualified professional.
