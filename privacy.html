Privacy Policy

Payperity · Last updated October 31, 2026
SupportPrivacy Policy

Payperity ("the app") is published by Khai M. King. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What the app collects

Nothing. Payperity has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not collect, sell or share personal data.

Where your information is stored

Everything you enter (income, expenses, debts, goals, your spending log and any bank file you import) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Exports and backups

When you tap an export or backup button, the app creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on) is your choice, and is handled by that service under its own privacy policy.

Network use

The app works offline. To keep tax figures current, it downloads a public tax-rate file from GitHub. That request contains none of your information. GitHub, like any web host, may log standard connection details such as IP address under its own privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or remove its data in Settings. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
